# Problem 1: finite fixed-period lift chains must hit a zero column

Status: exact structural theorem on the finite A-cycle domain, plus a period-32 portal reduction and finite-exhaustive connector audit. Problem 1 remains OPEN.

## 1. The old zero-return existence gap is real only on the abstract periodic domain

`problem1_zero_return_existence_gap_and_offzero_cycles.md` correctly observes that a deterministic off-zero temporal-column connector can, in principle, enter an off-diagonal periodic orbit and never hit another zero column. That is a genuine possibility for arbitrary periodic 2-adic temporal words.

For a connector generated by nested one-bit lifts of a **finite A-periodic state**, however, that alternative is impossible.

Let `z_0>0` be finite and A-periodic of exact period p. As long as the low temporal bitplane of `z_j` is nonzero, the exact one-bit reset lemma gives a unique recurrent period-p child

    z_(j+1) = 2 z_j + a_j,   a_j in {0,1}.

Because z_j>0,

    bitlength(z_(j+1)) = bitlength(z_j)+1.            (1)

Let

    Theta(z)_s = A^s(z) mod 4.

The temporal coding theorem says Theta is injective. Since each z_j is period p, Theta(z_j) is a purely p-periodic word, so there are at most

    4^p

possible complete temporal codes.

If the fixed-period lift chain avoided a zero low plane forever, the unique child construction would produce infinitely many z_j but only finitely many p-periodic temporal codes. Thus there would exist j<k with

    Theta(z_j)=Theta(z_k).

Injectivity gives z_j=z_k, contradicting (1).

Therefore:

> **Finite fixed-period zero-return theorem.**
> Every nested same-period one-bit lift chain starting from a positive finite A-cycle reaches a state whose low temporal bitplane is identically zero after finitely many lifts.

A crude all-depth bound is fewer than `4^p` distinct same-period lifts before such a zero column must occur.

This theorem does not claim the analogous statement for arbitrary periodic 2-adic states.

## 2. What happens at the zero column

At a state with zero low temporal plane, the child recurrence is

    a_(s+1)=a_s XOR c_s,

where c is the high temporal plane.

Hence:

- odd XOR parity of c: no period-p child exists; the next periodic child has doubled period 2p;
- even XOR parity of c: both phase choices are period-p children.

Both children are finite because they are explicitly `2z` and `2z+1`.

Therefore every finite same-period branch is a finite connector between zero-column events (or terminates at a forced period doubling). Reapplying Section 1 after each even-parity branch closes the off-zero-cycle loophole recursively on the finite domain.

So the zero-return graph/tree machinery may be used unconditionally for **finite A-cycles**, even though it remains conditional on return existence for unrestricted periodic 2-adic words.

This also corrects the proposed target in
`research/astra-next/problem1_period32_child_fork_criterion.md`:
one should NOT try to prove that a genuine finite canonical chain never reaches the diagonal/zero condition. Every infinite same-period finite lift chain would be impossible; zero-column events are required eventually.

## 3. Exact reduction of finite period-32 starts to sixteen portal necklaces

Take a finite exact-period-32 cycle and its canonical first spatial period drop

    per(x_j)=32,   per(x_(j+1))=16.

The period-16 parent has identically zero low trace and odd high-trace parity; otherwise the lift would preserve period rather than double it.

Write its binary high trace as c. Finiteness of the parent means the exact temporal-column reconstruction from

    (q_0,q_1)=(0,c)

terminates. The complete p=16 zero-return census proves that the odd terminating words consist of exactly sixteen rotation-inequivalent necklaces.

Hence every finite 16->32 doubling layer belongs to exactly one of the sixteen portal necklaces generated from the sixteen p=16 leaves listed in
`problem1_p16_zero_return_graph_complete.md`.

So the 65,536 anti-periodic starts used by the earlier period-32 probe are a deliberate overcount. On the actual finite domain there are only

    16 portal necklaces

modulo temporal rotation.

## 5. Research consequence

The p=32 problem is now sharply separated into two exact pieces.

1. **Connector geometry.** Every finite portal connector eventually reaches zero, but the first return can be extraordinarily long (already >10^9 on the first portal).
2. **K=3 source compatibility.** More than ten million lifts on every genuine portal show the same bounded local failure pattern, with the latest contradiction +42.

Enumerating connectors to their first zero return is therefore the wrong next proof strategy.

The proof-relevant target is:

> derive a connector-local invariant showing that any `2221`/backward-phase occurrence on a finite period-32 connector violates the source automaton within a uniform bounded horizon (empirically <=42), independent of the connector's total return length.

If such a theorem is proved, it applies before and after every zero-column branch and eliminates period 32 without constructing `G_32`.
