## Part A: accepted uniform finite-entry derivation for V (record)

Claim (scoped, rigorous-proof in this scope): fix one 2-adic x with sup_s V_s(x) <= K, where V_s(x)=sum_{t=0}^{s-1} bit_{2s}(T^t(x)) and T(x)=x XOR ((x<<1) OR (x<<2)). Then T^{h_V(K)}(x) is finitely supported, with h_V(K)=min{h: sum_{r=0}^{h} 1/(2r+1) > 3K/2}, h_V(0)=0.

Local rule and what commutes: u_i(t+1)=u_i(t) XOR (u_{i-1}(t) OR u_{i-2}(t)) at every interior index, and at every index of a bi-infinite row. Dependence is leftward: output bit i uses only input bits <=i, with coefficient 1 on bit i (unitriangular). Two notions must be distinguished. Spatial translations commute with T on bi-infinite rows by shift-invariance of the local rule, and finite-window continuity lets a fixed output window of an even-shift limit equal the limit of the corresponding input windows. By contrast the one-sided deletion pi (drop the low two bits) does NOT commute with T, and A=pi T does NOT commute with T; witnesses are recorded in Part B. No algebraic pi-T or A-T commutation is used or claimed anywhere in Part A. The only algebraic commutation invoked as a dependency for identities is pi A = A pi.

Even-shift limits carry at most K per even column. Extend x by zero at negative indices, take even shifts j_n=2m_n to infinity and a subsequential finite-window limit v (bi-infinite). For fixed output window and finite time only finitely many input cells matter, extending left at most 2 per step, so for large n the window lies above the artificial boundary and the limit row evolves by the same local rule. Fix an even limit offset 2a and a finite time set F; with s=m_n+a the limit column 2a is the limit of input columns 2s and horizon s-1 eventually exceeds max F. If the limit had K+1 ones in F, V_s(x) >= K+1 for large n, against hypothesis. Hence every even column of every even-shift limit has at most K ones over all t>=0, including t=0. Parity is essential: only even input columns are V-controlled, so only even shifts are used. No bound on the limit's own V is asserted.

Odd columns carry at most 2K, by inequality. From u_i(t+1) XOR u_i(t)=u_{i-1}(t) OR u_{i-2}(t), and A OR B >= A, A XOR B <= A+B: u_{i-1}(t) <= u_i(t)+u_i(t+1). This is an inequality, not an equality. If column i totals at most K, column i-1 totals at most 2K. So odd limit columns have at most 2K ones.

Last activity is rigid. With per-column finiteness, m_i=max{t:u_i(t)=1}, max(empty)=-infinity. For finite-support binary sequences last(u(t+1) XOR u(t))=m, including m=0 and the empty case. Hence m_i=max(m_{i-1},m_{i-2}) at every i in Z. Then m_i >= m_{i-1} and m_{i+1}=m_i, so all m_i share one value: all -infinity (zero spacetime) or all M>=0 with row M identically one and later rows zero.

Harmonic count gives 3K/2. In the nonzero case, at time M-r every length-(2r+1) interval holds a one, else its rightmost cell would stay zero after r updates by the left-expanding cone. Partitioning N consecutive columns yields sum_{r=0}^{M} floor(N/(2r+1)) <= (3K/2)N+O(K), since at most ceil(N/2) columns cost K and the rest cost 2K. The r-sum is finite, so dividing by N and letting N to infinity gives sum_{r=0}^{M} 1/(2r+1) <= 3K/2. By dyadic divergence h_V(K) is finite and M < h_V(K); for K=0 the spacetime is zero at time zero. One h_V(K) kills all even-shift limits.

From limits to the input. Let h=h_V(K), y=T^h(x). If y had infinite support at p_j to infinity, round each down to an even j_n, pass to constant offset d in {0,1} and a convergent shifted-input subsequence with limit v. Then bit_d(T^h(v))=lim bit_{j_n+d}(y)=1, since output (d,h) uses input window [d-2h,d], eventually above padding, against T^h(v)=0. Hence y is finitely supported. Nothing is claimed about extinction of the original one-sided columns.

## Part B: draft flaw confirmed and corrected isolation (independent verification)

Draft flaw (confirmed by hand). With y=1 (bit length B=1), z=17 (10001), a=v2(16)=4, the draft N=floor((a-B-1)/2)=1 asserts cells a-1=3 and a-2=2 both zero at times 0 and 1. At t=0 they are zero, but Tz=119=1110111 has bit 2 equal 1 at t=1. The lower cone agrees with y, yet y itself evolves: bitlen(T^t(y)) <= B+2t=3 at t=1, which forbids nonzero bits at indices >=3 but permits index 2. The draft bound was one step too weak. The statement as written is false; the hand case refutes it without any machine search.

Correction (verified). Put N=floor((a-B-2)/2). Let y be finite of bit length B (B=0 means y=0), z!=y with a=v2(z-y)>=B. Then z,y agree below a at t=0, and by causality bits below a agree for all t, since output bit i<a uses only input bits <=i<a. Hence cells a-1,a-2 follow T^t(y), whose support is below B+2t. They vanish whenever a-2 >= B+2t, i.e. t <= (a-B-2)/2, hence for t=0..N when N>=0. For large a this gives a>=2 so the interior rule applies. The hand case now gives N=floor((4-1-2)/2)=0, asserting only t=0, which is true.

While neighbors vanish, cell a stays one: u_a(t+1)=u_a(t) XOR 0, so u_a=1 for t=0..N+1. Cell a+1 (for a>=1) toggles each step since u_a OR u_{a-1}=1: u_{a+1}(t+1)=NOT u_{a+1}(t). Over the N+1 times 0..N it therefore holds at least floor((N+1)/2) ones regardless of starting value.

Activity contradiction with threshold a>=B+4L+4 (verified). If a is even, s=a/2 samples column a over 0..s-1; since N=floor((a-B-2)/2) <= s-1, V_s(z) >= N+1. If a is odd, s=(a+1)/2 samples column a+1 over 0..s-1; since N <= s-1 likewise, V_s(z) >= floor((N+1)/2). The odd case dominates: floor((N+1)/2) > L iff N+1 >= 2L+2 iff N >= 2L+1, and N=floor((a-B-2)/2) >= 2L+1 iff a >= B+4L+4. The even case follows since N+1 >= 2L+2 > L. Constants are nonsharp and valid; B=0 is included (then y=0 stays zero and the same counting holds, with small-a cases vacuous). No forced gate is used; this is about arbitrary T inputs. The estimate is uniform in the high bits above a by causality.

## Part B continued: time shift, first-difference preservation, compactness

Time shift adds at most h (verified). V_s(T^h(x))=sum_{t=0}^{s-1} bit_{2s}(T^{t+h}(x)) sums r=h..s-1+h versus 0..s-1 for V_s(x); at most h new times enter (exactly s terms when s<h, still at most h). So V_s(T^h(x)) <= V_s(x)+h, including s<h. With h=h_V(K) from Part A, T^h(E_K) has all-time bound L=K+h, where E_K={x: V_s(x)<=K all s>=0} (V_0=0). E_K is closed in Z_2 since each V_s uses finitely many digits, hence compact. Part A supplies this common entry time h; it supplies no explicit spatial-support formula.

T preserves the first difference (verified, rechecked after the commutation correction: this uses only unitriangularity, not any commutation). Output bit i equals input bit i XOR a function of strictly lower bits, so by induction on i, inputs agreeing below a give outputs agreeing below a, and differing at a give outputs differing at a. By induction on time, T^h preserves a=v2(x-x-prime) for 2-adic inputs. Fix x in E_K, y=T^h(x) finite of bit length B. If x-prime in E_K is close, a is the same for the pair (z=T^h(x-prime), y), and a can be made arbitrarily large. The corrected isolation estimate contradicts the common bound L unless x-prime=x once a>=B+4L+4. Each x hence has a binary cylinder meeting E_K only in x: E_K is discrete in itself. Compact plus discrete gives finite: cover E_K by such singleton-intersection cylinders, take a finite subcover, each member carries one point of E_K.

## Part B continued: rejected commutation, valid identity (2), rechecked (3) and base E_0 over all 2-adics

Rejected claim (explicit). The prior version of this review asserted pi commutes with T and A commutes with T. Both are false. Hand witnesses with x=1: T(1)=1 XOR (2 OR 4)=7, pi(1)=0, so pi(T(1))=pi(7)=1 whereas T(pi(1))=T(0)=0. Further A(1)=pi(T(1))=1, so T(A(1))=T(1)=7, whereas A(T(1))=A(7)=6 since T(7)=25 and pi(25)=6. Hence pi T != T pi and A T != T A. Every derivation in this file that invoked those equalities is withdrawn. The only algebraic commutation used as a dependency in this file is pi A = A pi.

Valid identity (2) via A-diagonal (verified, independent). For s>=1 the single-column note gives V_s(x)=sum_{t=0}^{s-1} bit_0(A^t(pi^{s-t}(x))), using only pi A=A pi as dependency. Then V_s(Ax)=sum_{t=0}^{s-1} bit_0(A^t(pi^{s-t}(Ax))). Since pi^{k}A=A pi^{k} from pi A=A pi, pi^{s-t}(Ax)=A(pi^{s-t}(x)), so each summand is bit_0(A^{t+1}(pi^{s-t}(x))). With r=t+1 this is sum_{r=1}^{s} bit_0(A^r(pi^{(s+1)-r}(x))). Against V_{s+1}(x)=sum_{r=0}^{s} bit_0(A^r(pi^{(s+1)-r}(x))), the difference is exactly the r=0 term bit_0(pi^{s+1}(x))=bit_{2s+2}(x). Hence V_s(Ax)=V_{s+1}(x)-bit_{2s+2}(x). Conclusion (2) stands on this route; A maps E_K into itself. No pi-T or A-T commutation is used.

Identity (3) rechecked (verified). For any G in {T,U,P} with pi G=A, and s>=1, every exponent s-t in the diagonal sum satisfies s-t>=1, so pi^{s-t}(Gx)=pi^{s-t-1}(Ax), then A^t pi^{s-t-1} A=A^{t+1} pi^{s-t-1} using only pi A=A pi. This yields sum_{r=1}^{s} bit_0(A^r pi^{s-r}(x)) with r=t+1, i.e. V_s(Gx)=V_s(x)-bit_{2s}(x)+bit_0(A^s(x)), independent of the generator. Recheck confirms no pi-T or A-T commutation enters: the only commutations are pi-G-to-A by hypothesis and pi-A. Hence G(E_K) lies in E_{K+1}, and with F=Q(Ax) only F(E_K intersect domain) lies in E_{K+1}. The level may grow at each forced step; finiteness of each E_K does not bound any F orbit and supplies no uniform graph or return potential. No such import is made here.

Base E_0={0,1,2,3} over all of Z_2 (verified; corrected full 2-adic argument). Let x be an arbitrary 2-adic input; no ordering and no higher-bits-zero hypothesis is used. If bit_{2s}(x)=1 for some s>=1, then V_s(x)>=1 at t=0 and x is not in E_0. Otherwise ALL even bits at indices >=2 are zero. If for some s>=1 an odd bit bit_{2s+1}(x)=1, then bit_{2s+2}(x)=0 by the even-case conclusion (whatever the higher bits above 2s+2 may be, even or odd), while bit_{2s+2}(T(x)) = 0 XOR (1 OR 0) = 1 using only bits at positions <=2s+2, so V_{s+1}(x)>=1 at t=1 (the sampled ages 0..s with s>=1 include t=1). Higher bits, including arbitrary further odd ones above, do not affect these two evaluated cells. Hence every 2-adic with any nonzero bit at index >=2 lies outside E_0. The remaining 2-adics have all bits >=2 zero, i.e. x in {0,1,2,3}, and these have V=0: with bit length B<=2, bitlen(T^t(x))<=B+2t forces bit_{2s}(T^t(x))=0 for t<=s-1 since 2s>=B+2t there, with B=0 (x=0) staying zero. So E_0 is exactly {0,1,2,3} in Z_2. This uses only the support-growth bound and the local rule, not any commutation.
