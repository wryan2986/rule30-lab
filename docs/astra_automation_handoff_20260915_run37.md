## New exact computational structure

Re-grouping the same exact finite-extension census by bitlength reveals a much stronger pattern:

- bitlengths 1..3: exactly one periodic state per length, period 1;
- bitlengths 4..8: exactly one 2-cycle per length (2 periodic states per length);
- bitlengths 9..29: exactly one 4-cycle per length (4 periodic states per length);
- bitlengths 30..400: exactly one 8-cycle per length (8 periodic states per length).

Thus every bitlength through 400 has exactly one periodic orbit, and its period is a power of two. The run-36 counts decompose exactly as `3=3*1`, `10=5*2`, `84=21*4`, `2968=371*8`.

The observed period-threshold starts are `1,4,9,30,401`. Length 401 has no period <=8 point, but period 16 there is not yet proved.

This strengthens the next theorem target: prove that for each bitlength the finite dynamical system induced by A has a unique periodic orbit; then prove that this unique orbit has power-of-two length. A leading-edge disagreement/coalescence argument may be more tractable than attacking period arithmetic directly.
