## New result

Let `r=R-2`, `L=O_R`, and

\[
f_r(t)=x_{r-1}(t)\lor x_{r-2}(t).
\]

Then

\[
D_{e_r}H_r^{(R)}\equiv0.
\]

So the exact derivative suggested by run 232 is a dead end: flipping the initial `x_r` complements `x_r(t)` for all times, and the even number `L/2` of samples cancels.

More usefully, direct expansion gives the exact all-depth identity

\[
\boxed{
H_r^{(R)}=
\bigoplus_{k=0}^{L/4-1}
\left(f_r(4k)\oplus f_r(4k+1)\right).
}
\]

Thus a plateau forcing `H_r^{(R)}=0` forces an all-state parity cancellation of adjacent forcing pairs in every four-step block.

## Interpretation

The hoped-for descent is not simply full-time parity -> even-time parity -> quarter-time parity. The next mask is the periodic dyadic mask `1100`: keep forcing times congruent to 0 or 1 mod 4. This points toward a time-mask/Walsh formulation rather than explicit expansion of `T^(2^q)`.
